About Dexchad

A Story Built
on Conviction.

My name is Precious Salvation — a Computer Science graduate from Rivers State University, and the person behind the Dexchad brand. I didn't enter Web3 chasing trends. I entered because I saw a future being built in real-time, and I made the decision to be one of the builders — not a spectator.

What started as curiosity about decentralized systems and blockchain technology in 2020 evolved into a mission: to bridge the gap between technology, finance, and opportunity — especially for young Africans positioned to win in the digital economy.

I combine a technical Computer Science background with deep expertise in blockchain, trading, digital finance, and community development. The result is a rare perspective — someone who understands the architecture, reads the markets, and builds the communities that make it all move.

Today, as Founder of Salvation Energy and Co-Founder of BlockchainRSU and MetaReach, I operate at the intersection of technology, finance, influence, and innovation — helping investors find clarity, traders stay sharp, and projects reach the right people.
